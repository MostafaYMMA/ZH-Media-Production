Before you ask

How it actually works

What happens after I book a call?

We hop on a short call to learn your coaching, your audience, and your goals. If it's a fit, we plan your first month of content and book your shoot — usually within the same week.

Do I have to travel to film?

No. We film on-location at your gym. If you're outside our area we run remote shoots and direct you over the call — many coaches capture a full month in one session.

How many reels, and how fast?

12 or 24 finished reels a month, depending on your package. First edits usually land within a few days of the shoot — captioned and ready to post.

What if I want changes to an edit?

Every reel includes revisions. We tweak the cut, captions, or pacing until it's something you're happy to put your name on.

Am I locked into a long contract?

We work month to month. The plan is that the results keep you around — not the paperwork.

How much is the investment?

Your investment depends on your package and shoot logistics, so we quote it on the call once we know what you need. Book a call and we'll walk you through it.

Do you post the reels for me?

We handle the script, the shoot, and the edit, and deliver every reel ready to post. You post them to your own accounts, so your page stays fully yours.
